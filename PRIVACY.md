# Nijatva Dialer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nijatva Dialer is a private, offline phone dialer, contacts and SMS app for Android.

## Data We Collect

**None.** Nijatva Dialer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has **no internet permission** — it cannot send data anywhere. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Settings (speed dial assignments, blocked numbers, appearance preferences) are stored in Android's local app storage on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is removed when you uninstall the app, and is not accessible to other apps. Android backup is explicitly disabled (`allowBackup="false"`).

## Permissions

- **Phone / SMS** — required to function as your dialer and messaging app
- **Contacts** — to display and manage contacts
- **Storage / Media** — for backup-restore and call recording playback
- No location, no camera, no microphone, no internet

For privacy questions, open an issue on this repository.
